WHO I AM

Brief Autobiography

I was born 33 years ago in Ganta City, Nimba County, Liberia, to the union of Mr. Konah Gaye and Madam Marie Zigbuo. I began my early childhood education in Sanniquellie City, Nimba County, where I attended pre-primary elementary school.

I later enrolled at Martha Tubman Elementary School, where I successfully completed my primary education. Following my primary education, I attended Sanniquellie Central Junior and Senior High School.

In 2011, I migrated to Montserrado County to further my education and enrolled at Nathan E. Gibson Memorial Academy, where I continued and completed my secondary education.

Driven by a passion for learning and public service, I am currently pursuing two university degrees. I am a student at Starz University, where I study Information Technology with a major in System Administration. I am also a student at the University of Liberia, pursuing a degree in Public Administration.

Throughout my life, I have demonstrated a strong passion for leadership, community service, youth empowerment, and humanitarian work. My commitment to serving humanity has earned me respect within my community and beyond.

I have actively participated in educational, social, and community development initiatives and remain dedicated to inspiring young people to become responsible, prepared, and visionary leaders.

I am the Founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Konah's Educational Foundation, an organization that has impacted many lives through Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) and educational empowerment programs.

I also serve as the Chairman of The Intellectual Class of Paynesville (ICP), one of Paynesville's advocacy movements. I previously served as Chairman of the A.B. Tolbert Road Block-C Community, where I championed community development and citizen engagement.

In 2023, I contested as a Representative Candidate for Electoral District #5, Montserrado County, demonstrating my commitment to democratic governance and public service.

Today, I continue to serve as a youth advocate, humanitarian, educator, and community leader committed to promoting education, leadership, and national development in Liberia.
